BYU Rocketry

I started in the BYU Rocketry club by participating in the Student Launch Competition. In the Student Launch Competition, teams design, build, and launch a small model rocket (H motor) with the aim to accurately hit a target altitude.

Following my participation in the Student Launch Competition, I took the opportunity to design and construct my own rocket. My rocket is designed to be suitable for L1 and L2 certification flights and has been launched three times.

In the Fall of 2024, I was selected to be the team lead for the Guidance, Navigation, and Control Research and Development (GNC R&D) team. This team focuses on developing the next generation of GNC systems for BYU Rocketry's larger rockets. Currently, we are working on developing a new flight computer with general compatibility with the club's rockets and RTK GPS capabilities as well as aerobreaking.
