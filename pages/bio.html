My Childhood

I was born as the first child in my family, and shortly after my birth, my parents and I moved to a small town in Northern Nevada, about an hour away from Reno. I lived there all the way through high school and participated in many activities. I played soccer in elementary school and joined Boy Scouts of America when I turned 8. In middle school I started running cross country and track and I also competed in Mathcounts, a math competition. In high school I continued to run, and I also participated in Academic Team, golf, and NHS. Thanks to a dual enrollment program, I graduated from high school with an Associates of Science. And that same spring, I became an Eagle Scout.

Proseliting Mission

After graduating, it was my goal to serve a two year proseliting mission for my church. In order to pay for it, I worked for a year after graduation. Some of that time was spent working as a handyman, fixing houses, and the rest of that time was spent working in a warehouse that was closer to home.

I was finally ready to leave early in the year 2020. In fact, I received my assignment to serve just as quarentine started. My original assignment was to Buenos Aires, Argentina, but in light of the pandemic, I was temporarily reassigned to the midwest. I ended up spending 16 months in Iowa, Minnesota, South Dakota, and Nebraska before I could go to Argentina. In Argentina, I spent nearly all of my time serving in the administrative offices for the mission. Part of that time was as the Financial Secretary, but for the majority of my time in the offices, I was the Technology Secretary. As the Technology Secretrary, I helped develop a new ad campaign for the mission, give trainings, and keep the mission phones supplied and in good condition.

College/Present

I was fortunate enough to be accepted into Brigham Young University in Provo, Utah; and I came here to study right after my mission. I am studying Mechanical Engineering with an empahsis in Aerospace along with a minor in Computer Science.

At BYU, I am an active member in the Rocketry Club. This fall (2024), I was selected to be the team lead for the GNC R&D team, and we are working on developing the next set of tools to be used by the club's high power team.

Both last year ('23-'24), and this year ('24-'25), I have been a competitor in BYU's Student Innovator Of the Year (SIOY) Competition. My team and I are working to develop a portable contraption to make photogrammetry much more feasible by taking multiple pictures at once.
